# How upanishad.hr is written

Rules for every text on the site, Croatian and English. They come from NN/g, GOV.UK, the EU's "Pišimo jasno",
Julian Shapiro, April Dunford, Evil Martians' study of 100 developer-tool landing pages and Wikipedia's
"Signs of AI writing" (research notes, October 2026).

## What to say

- The headline says what we sell. Someone who reads only the header knows what Upanishad does.
- Say how we work. That is our proof: previews in minutes, nothing changes without confirmation,
  documentation the build keeps honest, no manual steps, prototype before code.
- Every claim has proof next to it: a named client, a real mechanism, a link. If it can't be shown, cut it.
- Numbers only when the buyer would use them (minutes to a preview, latency, hours saved). No vanity counts
  (commits, ADRs, plugins, "AI wrote X %").
- Be exact about project stage: Aura is in production, SportLive is ready for pilot, OTISAK is a proof of
  concept, KLIMA is in preparation.
- No promises without scope: no fixed timelines, no availability promises ("we're there at night").
- Never state headcount. Show Matej (CEO i arhitekt) and Leo (CTO).
- Never name: Striker/Polymarket, scrapers, feed proxies, Only Entertain. Catapult/Impect: describe the work
  in general terms; results belong to the client.

## How to say it

- First person plural, the reader is "vi" (lower case). In Croatian, drop "mi"; the verb carries it.
- Verbs, not nouns: "odlučiti", not "donijeti odluku"; "provjeravamo", not "vršimo provjeru".
- Active voice, name who acts. Sentences under ~20 words on average, mix short and longer.
  One idea per paragraph, point first.
- Write the Croatian natively, not as a translation. Prefer Croatian words where they exist
  (provedba, povratna informacija); keep "feed", "AI", "API" for the technical reader.
- Plain "je/ima" instead of "predstavlja/nudi/služi kao".
- Calls to action name the next step: "Opišite nam projekt", not "Razgovarajmo" or "Get started".
- Nature and the river live in the visuals. In text, at most one literal line (Aljmaš, the Drava meets the
  Danube). No metaphors that need decoding (dams, tributaries, spawning).

## Don't (AI tells)

- "Nije samo X, nego Y", "It's not X, it's Y".
- Lists of three everywhere ("brzo, pouzdano, skalabilno").
- Trailing clauses that fake analysis: "…, čime osiguravamo…", "…, ensuring reliability".
- Puffery: inovativan, napredan, vrhunski, pametan, moćan, rješenja, bešavno, ključan;
  seamless, robust, leverage, empower, cutting-edge, pivotal, testament, landscape.
- Em dashes as decoration; title case headings; bold-label bullets.
- Slogans that sound good and say nothing. Read the text aloud. If a buyer would ask "meaning what?", rewrite.
